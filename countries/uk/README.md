Farming policy is devolved. England, Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland
have different agencies, schemes, and some different rules. This page leans
towards England. Sections for the other nations are welcome.

## Who runs what

| Body | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Defra | Government department for farming in England. |
| Rural Payments Agency (RPA) | Pays subsidies in England. Issues holding (CPH) numbers. |
| Animal and Plant Health Agency (APHA) | Animal registration, disease control, bird flu rules. |
| Food Standards Agency (FSA) | Food safety. Abattoirs. Raw milk. |
| Your local council | Planning permission. Food business registration. Allotments. |
| HMRC | Tax. |
| Environment Agency | Water abstraction and pollution in England. |

## Planning

- You need planning permission to build a house on farmland. It is hard to
  get.
- Farms have some "permitted development" rights. These allow certain farm
  buildings and works without a full application. The rights depend on the
  size of the holding. Holdings of 5 hectares or more get the most. Small
  holdings get fewer. <!-- TODO: needs source — current thresholds and limits -->
- Polytunnels, sheds, tracks, and caravans can all need permission. Ask the

## Registering animals

- **Holding number.** To keep sheep, goats, pigs, or cattle, you need a
  County Parish Holding (CPH) number for your land. In England it comes from
  the RPA. You need it before the animals arrive.
- **Sheep and goats.** Register as a keeper with APHA to get a flock or herd
  mark. Animals must be tagged. You must keep a holding register and report
  movements. <!-- TODO: needs source — link to the current rules and the movement reporting service for each nation -->
- **Poultry.** All bird keepers in Great Britain must register their birds,
  even one hen in a garden. This has applied in England and Wales since
  1 October 2024, and in Scotland since 1 September 2024.
  <!-- TODO: needs source link -->
- **Bird flu.** APHA can declare prevention zones and housing orders. You
  must then follow biosecurity rules and may have to keep birds under cover.
- **Bees.** Registration on BeeBase, run by the National Bee Unit, is free
  and recommended. Some bee diseases are notifiable by law.
- **Medicine records.** Keepers of food animals must record the medicines
  they give. <!-- TODO: needs source -->

## Selling food

- **Food business registration.** If you sell food, register with your local
  council at least 28 days before you start. It is free.
- **Eggs.** Small flocks can sell direct to customers with few rules. Rules
  get stricter at 50 birds or more, and when you sell to shops.

## Tax notes

- **Trading allowance.** You can earn up to £1,000 a year from trading
  before you need to tell HMRC. Above that, register for Self Assessment.
- **VAT.** Most food is zero-rated. You must register for VAT when your
  taxable turnover passes £90,000 (threshold since April 2024).
- **Hobby or business.** HMRC looks at whether you trade on a commercial
  basis with a view to profit. This affects whether you can claim losses.
