# 03: First things (pick your first deploy)

One page for each thing a beginner might start with. Every page uses the same
[template](../../templates/guide-page.md), so you can compare them.

| Guide | Cost to start | Daily work? | Time to first output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Vegetables](vegetables.md) | Low | No | 4 to 8 weeks |
| [Fruit](fruit.md) | Low | No | 1 to 8 years |
| [Chickens](chickens.md) | Low to medium | Yes | 2 to 6 weeks |
| [Bees](bees.md) | Medium | No, weekly in season | Often year 2 |
| [Goats](goats.md) | Medium to high | Yes, twice a day if milking | 1 to 2 years |
| [Sheep](sheep.md) | Medium to high | Yes | About 1 year |

## Suggested order

1. Vegetables. Cheap, fast, and forgiving.
2. Fruit. Plant in your first winter, because the wait is long.
3. Chickens. Your first animals. They teach you daily routine.
4. Bees, goats, or sheep. Only after a year of the above.

Add one new thing per year.
