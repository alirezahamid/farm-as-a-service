# 04: Tools and equipment (your dev environment)

You need less than you think. Buy for the job in front of you, not the farm
in your head.

## Rules for buying

1. **Buy used.** Old hand tools are often better steel than new cheap ones.
   Look at farm sales, auctions, and online listings.
2. **Borrow or hire first.** If you use it once a year, do not own it.
3. **Buy the good version of what you use daily.** A cheap spade bends in
   week two.
4. **Wait a season.** If you still need it after a year of working around
   it, buy it.

## Year-one list (the minimum viable shed)

### For growing

| Tool | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Spade | Digging, planting, edging. |
| Digging fork | Loosening soil, lifting roots, turning compost. |
| Hoe | Weeding. The tool you will use most. |
| Rake | Levelling seed beds. |
| Hand trowel | Planting out. |
| Secateurs | Pruning and harvesting. |
| Wheelbarrow | Moving compost, muck, and everything else. |
| Watering can and hose | Seedlings need a fine rose on the can. |
| Buckets | You never have enough. |
| String line and a tape measure | Straight rows are easier to hoe. |
| Gloves and boots | Waterproof boots with a real sole. |

### For animals

| Item | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Feed bins with tight lids | Metal is best. Rats chew plastic. |
| Drinkers and troughs | One spare for when one cracks in frost. |
| Fencing tools | Fencing pliers, a hammer, staples, and a post rammer. |
| Electric fence energiser and tester | If you use electric netting or strands. |
| Head torch | Winter chores happen in the dark. |
| Animal first aid kit | Ask your vet what belongs in it. |

### For you

| Item | Why |
| --- | --- |
| First aid kit | In the shed, not in the house. |
| Phone on your body | You will often work alone. |
| Up-to-date tetanus vaccine | Soil and rusty wire. Ask your doctor. |
| Waterproofs | Good ones. You will live in them. |
| A notebook | See [Farm tech](06-farm-tech.md). Records start on day one. |

## What can wait (the backlog)

| Item | Why it can wait |
| --- | --- |
| Tractor | Under about 2 ha (5 acres), hire a contractor for the few big jobs. |
| Rotavator | Hire one for a day. Or use no-dig beds and skip it. |
| Polytunnel or greenhouse | Very useful, but learn outdoors first. Then you know where to put it. |
| Quad bike or UTV | Walk. You see more on foot. |
| Chainsaw | Wait until you have training and safety gear. A bow saw works on small wood. |
| Livestock trailer | Borrow, or pay a neighbour, until you move animals often. |
| Milking machine | Hand milk until you have more than a few animals. |
| Honey extractor | Borrow from your local beekeeping association. |
| Irrigation system | A hose works for the first year. Then you know where the pipes should go. |
| Anything from a video | The influencer got it for free. |

## Safety (please do not skip the tests)

Farm tools injure and kill people every year. See the
[Reality check](00-reality-check.md).

- Get training before you use a chainsaw, a tractor, or a quad.
- Wear the safety gear. Chainsaw trousers, boots, helmet, and visor.
- Never bypass a guard on a machine. Stop the engine before you clear a
  blockage.
- Tell someone where you are when you work alone with machines or large
  animals.
